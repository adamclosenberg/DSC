Export to Pastel
Pulls all Confirmed orders from Airtable, generates the Pastel Express v19 CSV import file, and saves it to Google Drive.
Say "export orders to Pastel" in Claude → CSV saved to Google Drive → import via Process › Customers › Batch › Import in Pastel Express v19.

Generate Cut Sheets
Aggregates order lines by style+colourway, calculates size breakdowns and fabric requirements, outputs a PDF pack to Google Drive.
Say "generate cut sheets" in Claude → one PDF per style+colourway, sizes aggregated across all orders, saved to Google Drive.
